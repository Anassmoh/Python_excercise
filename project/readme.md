Cast your magnet and collect metal litter to your net. recycle it manually from the menu, or automatically by quiting or restarting the game.


The magnet is equiped with a metal detector that beeps in a higher frequency if an item is actively hooked to your magnet.

You can choose to play in the roadside where you can find item such as nails, brake pads, coins.., or in the beach where you can find items such as rings, fish tins , beverage cans..., you can also choose to cast your magnet in the lake, a classic place where you can find fishing hooks, bikes, and sometimes evens weapons!

Open your inventory to check your progress, you will start the game with 3 empty seperate nets.
