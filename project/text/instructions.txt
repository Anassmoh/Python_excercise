You are now equiped with MagMed and 3 MagNets! They are reinforced heavy-duty nets for collecting 
corroded metal.
Choose one of the litter hotspots and start casting your MagMed! Once you detect a metal item,
scrap the ground surface and pick it up, an iron item will naturally be pulled by MagMed. Proceed 
to measure its weight, and write down what it is. The item is collected to the relevant MagNet, 
your catch is then displayed in the inventory.
Each litter zone has its own MagNet, you need to recycle its content after it starts to fill up.
you can also recycle all MagNets at once.
Your goal is to earn 2500 Markkaa, the recycling revenue depends on the total weight of each matter.
gold being the most rewarding.
Save the progress by properly quitting the game, the wallet will be saved as it is in the menu,
all the non-recycled items will be processed automatically without you earning its worth.
Remember! your MagMed is battery powered, it's consumed while actively magnet fishing. Draining it 
will lead to an improper game shutdown, make sure you casually recycle your catch, then quit the 
game once the battery is low, this will save your progress and allow you to recharge the battery.
Choosing to restart the game however, will delete your account as well as the progress.
