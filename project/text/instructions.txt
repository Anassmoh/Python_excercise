You are now equiped with MagMed and 3 MagNets, they are reinforced heavy duty-nets for collecting 
corroded metal.
Choose one of the litter hotspots and start casting your MagMed, once you detect a metal item,
scrap the ground and pick it up, an iron item however will be pulled by MagMed. Proceed to measure 
its weight, and write down what it is. The item is collected the to relevant MagNet, your catch is then 
displayed in the inventory.
Each litter zone has it own MagNet, which you need recycle its content after it starts to fill up.
you can also recycle all 3 MagNets at once.
Your goal is to earn 2500 Markkaa, the recycle revenue depends on the total weight of each matter.
gold being the most rewarding.
Save the progress by properly quitting the game, the wallet will be saved as it is in the menu,
all the non-recycled items will be processed automatically without you earning its worth.
Remember that your MagMed is battery powered, it is consumed while actively magnet fishing. 
Draining it will lead to an unproper game shutdown, make sure you casually recycle your catch,
then quit the game once the battery is low. this will save your progress and allow you to recharge
the battery. You can also restart the game, which will delete your account as well as the progress.
These are some reported common finds according to the recycling center:
nail, screw, bolt, ring, bracelet, horseshoe, brake pad, lug nut, battery, wire, beverage can, 
watch, shell casing, munition, knife, necklace, fishing hook, coin, key..
