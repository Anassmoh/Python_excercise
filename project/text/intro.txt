The land mines have been swept for the first time after the war, villagers are left in surprise as the 
infantry units depart with hundreds of undetonated anti-tank mines.
You watch the sappers from the bridge over the lake taking their helmets off, your thoughts attempting
to replace your nostalgic memories of a childhood playground with the reality of what it was, a minefield!
What secrets does the sand hide? What stories lie beneath this lake?
But you don't let curiosity hunt you, you hunt curiosity!
Equipe yourself with MagMed, a home made magnet fused with a metal detector, and sweep the area!
